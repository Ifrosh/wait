The day-to-day reality

How selling works for you.

Your location, scale, season and trading relationships matter. We want to hear what actually happens in your context.

  • How do you usually find new buyers?
  • How do you decide on a fair price and minimum quantity?
  • How do you confirm that a buyer will follow through?
  • Who arranges transport, and how is the cost agreed?
  • What happens if a buyer delays, changes the order or rejects produce?

Understanding what already works is just as important as finding gaps. These are research questions, not claims that AMIFY already solves them.
