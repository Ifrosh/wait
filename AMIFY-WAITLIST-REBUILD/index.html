Agriculture, built with people

Food moves through people. Let's make the journey work better.

From finding the right trading partner to agreeing on quality, price and delivery, agricultural trade can be complicated. AMIFY is listening to the people who experience it every day — before deciding what to build.

The bigger picture

Better trade starts with better questions.

We don't want to assume we know the biggest problems. We want to understand what people already do, what it costs them, and where the process breaks down.

Getting the goods there

A practical approach

We want to understand the way things work today.

That means learning from real experiences — including the workarounds people rely on and the parts that already work well.

  • Learn from farmers, producers, buyers and food businesses.
  • Understand the frequency and cost of everyday challenges.
  • Explore which improvements people would genuinely use.
  • Test ideas with the community before making big promises.
